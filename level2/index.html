Jimothy Level 2 — climb the Space Needle (prototype)

Prototype second level of the Jimothy game. Jimothy the round Seattle raccoon climbs the Space Needle on a grey-then-golden Pacific Northwest day — beam by beam past the hourglass legs, riding the golden elevator, rummaging ironworker lunchboxes and seagull nests, with Mount Rainier and a departing ferry on Elliott Bay below. Play level 1 at playjimothy.com.
