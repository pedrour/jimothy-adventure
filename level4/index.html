The Seattle Underground game — explore the buried city as Jimothy the raccoon

A free Seattle Underground game, and level 4 of Jimothy Eats Seattle. Jimothy, Seattle's viral round raccoon, starts at the Pioneer Square pergola at dusk, passes the totem pole and the five-globe lamps, and takes the stairs down into the real Seattle Underground — the streets buried when the city rebuilt 10 to 35 feet higher after the Great Fire of June 6, 1889. Under the famous purple glass sidewalk prisms he raids the old bank vault, a hotel lobby, the barbershop, the elevated high-tide crapper and a speakeasy, dodges rats and moles, meets Gerald and Bianca of the underground raccoon camp, and collects seven true history plaques — from the glue-pot fire to Bill Speidel's 1965 underground tour — before climbing out at the tour exit.

How to play the Seattle Underground game

Arrow keys or on-screen buttons to move and jump, S or the down button to rummage. Jump the rat packs, step back when a mole mound starts to rumble, and read every plaque. Free in any browser on phone, tablet or desktop — no download, no sign-up.
