inspired by Seattle's real viral raccoon, Jimothy Piggerton

Jimothy Eats Seattle is a series of free browser games starring Jimothy Piggerton, the very round raccoon who went viral in Seattle. Play the original Jimothy raccoon game downtown, the Space Needle climbing game, or the new Pike Place Market game — dodge the famous flying fish, rub Rachel the bronze pig for luck and visit the gum wall. No download, no sign-up; works on phone, tablet and desktop.
