The Jimothy games, level by level

Jimothy Eats Seattle is a series of free browser games starring Jimothy Piggerton, the very round raccoon who went viral in Seattle. No download, no sign-up, no ads; every level works on phone, tablet and desktop.

Level 1 — Ballard Dash: the original Jimothy raccoon game. Dash through Ballard — the Seattle neighborhood the real Jimothy actually roams — raiding trash cans and snack carts beneath the skyline, with the Space Needle and the Great Wheel on the horizon.

Level 2 — Climb the Needle: a free Space Needle climbing game. Scale the real tower beam by beam, 605 feet past planes, drones and tourists to the observation deck, with Mount Rainier and Elliott Bay ferries in view.

Level 3 — Raid Pike Place: the Pike Place Market game. Dodge the famous flying fish at the fish stalls, rub Rachel the bronze pig for luck under the Public Market Center sign, browse flower, produce, bakery and spice stalls, and brave the fully-gummed Post Alley gum wall on the way to the bay.

Level 4 — The Underground: the new Seattle Underground game. Descend from the Pioneer Square pergola into the real buried city of 1889: purple glass sidewalk prisms, the old bank vault, a hotel lobby, a speakeasy and a raccoon camp — dodge rats and moles and collect seven true history plaques, from the glue-pot fire to Bill Speidel's 1965 tour.

Frequently asked questions

What is Jimothy Eats Seattle? A free browser platformer series starring Jimothy Piggerton, Seattle's viral round raccoon, across four levels: Ballard, the Space Needle, Pike Place Market and the Seattle Underground.

Is there a Seattle Underground game? Yes — level 4 is set in the real Underground beneath Pioneer Square, with its purple glass skylights and true history plaques.

Is the Jimothy game free? Yes — every level is completely free in any web browser. Supporters can optionally buy a $2 coffee on Ko-fi to unlock the Golden Jimothy skin in all levels.

Who is Jimothy Piggerton? A real, unusually round raccoon who became a Seattle celebrity; the games are a fan tribute set in his real stomping grounds.

How do I play? Arrow keys or on-screen buttons to move, Space or up to jump, S or the down button to rummage. Collect every snack, then reach the goal.
